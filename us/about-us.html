info Who we are

Invoice Luxe is a free progressive web app (PWA) built for people who need reliable business tools without subscriptions. Whether you issue invoices in the US or UK, calculate VAT, analyse tax, apply discounts or convert units, everything runs in your browser and can be installed on iPhone, Android or desktop for offline use.

Our mission is simple: give freelancers and SMEs the same quality tools that paid software offers — completely free, private, and fast.

construction What you can do

Invoice Luxe brings together the tools freelancers search for every day:

  • Free invoice generator — professional PDF invoices, client list and archive. No account required.
  • VAT calculator & sales tax calculator — split tax from gross or net amounts instantly (UK VAT, US sales tax and international rates).
  • Discount calculator — percentage off, sale price and savings in one step.
  • Tax analytics / VAT report — track tax to pay, tax to reclaim and net profit for clearer books.
  • Income & expense tracking — simple pre-accounting view of money in and out.
  • Work time tracker — log billable hours and turn them into invoices.
  • Unit converter & km calculator — distance, weight, temperature, speed, volume and more.
  • Sign PDF online, image to PDF, QR codes, password generator and other daily utilities.
