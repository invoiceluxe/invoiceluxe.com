info Who we are

Invoice Luxe is a progressive web app (PWA) that gives you the everyday tools of running a small business in one clean place. Instead of paying for a monthly subscription just to send an invoice or add up your hours, you open Invoice Luxe, pick a tool and get it done.

Our mission is simple: give self-employed people and small teams the quality of paid software — completely free, private and fast. Everything runs in your browser, and you can add it to your iPhone, Android or desktop home screen and use it like a regular app.

public Made for the US — and friendly for Canada & the UK

Our tools are written first for US users, with dollars, US date formats and US units. They also work well for self-employed people across North America and the UK, because you enter the tax rate that applies to you.

United States

Sales tax calculator, USD invoices, hourly pay and timesheets for W-2 style hourly work and 1099 contractor billing, plus a US unit converter (inches, feet, miles, pounds, gallons, Fahrenheit).

Canada

Enter your GST, HST or PST rate in the tax calculators and invoice in CAD. Rates vary by province, so use the one that applies to your sale.

United Kingdom

Use the VAT calculator to split VAT from gross or net prices and create GBP invoices for your clients. Check the current VAT rate for your goods or services.

Tax rates and rules differ by state, province and country and can change. Always confirm the right rate with your tax authority or a qualified professional.

rocket_launch How it works: start in 3 steps

  1. Pick a tool. Open the free tools page and use the search bar, or tap a favorite.
  2. Enter your details. Add your hours, prices, tax rate or client info. Nothing to sign up for.
  3. Download or share. Save a clean PDF, copy your result or keep it in your archive.

Install Invoice Luxe on your phone or computer

iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open in Chrome and tap Install app. Desktop: click the install icon in the Chrome or Edge address bar. Once installed, it opens full screen like any other app.

help Frequently asked questions

Is Invoice Luxe really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use. There is no signup, no watermark on your PDFs, no trial period and no credit card.

Is there a free invoice generator with no signup?

Yes. Open the Create Invoice tool, fill in your details and download a professional PDF invoice. No account is needed.

Can freelancers and 1099 contractors use Invoice Luxe?

Yes. It is built for freelancers, independent contractors, consultants, gig workers and small business owners who need to bill clients and track their hours.

Does it work for Canada and the UK?

Yes. You can enter the tax rate that applies to you, such as US sales tax, Canadian GST or HST, or UK VAT. Rates differ by region, so always confirm the correct rate for your sale.

Does Invoice Luxe work offline?

Yes. Install it as a Progressive Web App on iPhone, Android or desktop and use it like a regular app.

Where is my data stored?

Your data stays on your own device. You do not need to create an account to use the tools.

Is this tax or legal advice?

No. The tools are for general information only. For tax, legal or accounting questions, talk to a qualified professional.
