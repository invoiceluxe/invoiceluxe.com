How a VAT Calculator Calculates (Formulas)

An online VAT calculator uses two main formulas depending on whether the price already includes tax:

Gross → Net (tax included)
Net = Gross ÷ (1 + Rate)

Example: $108.25 ÷ 1.0825 = $100.00 net (8.25% tax)

Net → Gross (add tax)
Gross = Net × (1 + Rate)

Example: $100 × 1.0825 = $108.25 gross

Tax amount only
Tax = Gross − Net  or  Tax = Net × Rate

The free online VAT calculator on this page applies these formulas instantly as you type. You can also use the built-in calculator for any other math.

How Companies Create Invoices with VAT / Sales Tax

When a business issues an invoice, it typically shows three lines:

  1. Net (or subtotal) — the price before tax
  2. VAT / Sales Tax — calculated as Net × Rate
  3. Gross (or total) — Net + Tax, the amount the customer pays

Example invoice line:

Product A     Net $200.00
Sales tax 8.25%    $16.50
Total due      $216.50

Accounting software and e-commerce platforms (Shopify, Amazon, QuickBooks, Xero) use the same formulas as this free online VAT calculator. The seller collects the tax and later remits it to the state or tax authority. Buyers who can reclaim VAT (B2B in many countries) need the net amount clearly shown — that is why separating net, tax and gross matters.
