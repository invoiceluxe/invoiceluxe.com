Free online business tools for freelancers, contractors & small businesses in the US

Invoice Luxe is a free web app that puts the everyday tools of a freelancer, independent contractor or small business owner in one clean place: an invoice generator, a work hours and salary calculator, PDF tools, a sales tax calculator and more. There is no signup, no watermark and no credit card. Open a tool and get to work.

Free invoice generator & client management

Create a professional PDF invoice in minutes, keep a customer list, archive past invoices and track what has been paid. It is built for freelancers, gig workers, consultants, creatives, tradespeople and Etsy or online sellers who need to bill clients quickly without paying for accounting software.

Timesheet, work hours & paycheck calculator

Log the hours you work, set your hourly rate and see your pay totals instantly. Export a PDF timesheet to share with a client or employer. Handy for hourly workers, part-time jobs, shift work and contractors who bill by the hour. Use the minutes-to-seconds tool to convert time and decimal hours.

PDF editor, signer, OCR & image tools

  • Sign and crop PDF files right in your browser.
  • Image to PDF — turn JPG and PNG photos into a single document.
  • PDF OCR — pull text out of scanned PDFs.
  • Image resizer — change photo dimensions for email, web or forms.

Sales tax, discount & income tracking

Add or remove sales tax from a price, work out discounts and sale prices, calculate net price, and keep an eye on cash flow with the income and expense tracker. Sales tax rates differ by state and locality, so enter the rate that applies to your sale.

Everyday utilities

Generate a free QR code, create a strong random password, convert US units like inches, feet, pounds, ounces, gallons and Fahrenheit, build a ZIP file or use a quick calculator — all without installing anything.

Install it as an app on iPhone, Android & desktop

Invoice Luxe is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android: open in Chrome and tap Install app. On desktop Chrome or Edge: click the install icon in the address bar. Your data stays on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Are Invoice Luxe tools really free?

Yes. Every tool on this page is 100% free to use, with no signup and no credit card.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can open any tool and start right away. Your data stays on your own device.

How do I add Invoice Luxe to my home screen?

On iPhone, open the page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap Install app. On desktop Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar.

Can I create a PDF invoice as a freelancer or independent contractor?

Yes. The Create Invoice tool builds a professional PDF invoice you can send to clients. You can also keep a customer list and an archive of past invoices.

How do I calculate my pay from hours worked?

Use the Work Hours + Salary calculator. Log your hours, enter your hourly rate, and get pay totals plus an exportable PDF timesheet.

Is the sales tax calculator tax advice?

No. Sales tax rules and rates vary by state and locality, so the calculators are for general information only. Confirm the rate that applies to your sale with your state or a tax professional.
