What should a receipt include?

A good receipt shows the seller name and contact details, a unique receipt number, the date, what was bought with quantities and prices, any tax, the total and how it was paid. Keeping these details makes it easier for customers to claim returns and for you to prove income at tax time.

Receipt vs invoice

An invoice asks for payment that is still due. A receipt confirms that payment has already been made. If you still need to get paid, create an invoice instead.
