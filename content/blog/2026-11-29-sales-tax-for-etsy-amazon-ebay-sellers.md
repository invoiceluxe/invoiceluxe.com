If you sell on a marketplace such as Etsy, Amazon or eBay, you might wonder whether you need to deal with sales tax at all. The answer is "partly". Here is who collects what.

## What is a marketplace facilitator law?

A marketplace facilitator is a platform that lets third-party sellers list and sell products and handles the payment. Under marketplace facilitator laws, the **platform** must collect and send sales tax on orders placed through it, once it crosses the state's threshold. Every state that has a statewide sales tax, plus Washington, D.C., has this kind of law.

For you as a seller, this means the marketplace usually collects the tax at checkout and remits it to the state on those orders.

## What you may still need to do

Marketplace collection covers only orders the marketplace collects tax on. You are still responsible for:

- **Sales on your own website** or app
- **Wholesale and in-person sales,** such as craft fairs
- **Registration in your home state,** if your state requires a permit for any sales
- **Records** of your sales by state

## Why your marketplace sales may still matter

In some states, marketplace sales count toward **your** nexus threshold, even though the marketplace collected the tax. Add your marketplace sales and your direct sales together when you check each state's threshold. See [what is sales tax nexus](/blog/what-is-sales-tax-nexus/).

## A simple example

In one year you sell $90,000 on a marketplace to customers in one state and $25,000 from your own website to the same state. If that state counts both toward the $100,000 threshold, your total is $115,000. You may need to register and collect tax on your **own website** sales there, while the marketplace keeps collecting on its orders.

## How to stay organized

1. **Track sales by state** and by channel (marketplace vs your site).
2. **Compare totals** with each state's threshold at least every quarter.
3. **Register** when you cross a threshold. See [do I need a sales tax permit](/blog/do-i-need-a-sales-tax-permit/).
4. **File** your returns on time. See [how to file and pay sales tax](/blog/how-to-file-and-pay-sales-tax/).
5. **Invoice correctly** for direct sales. See [how to show sales tax on an invoice](/blog/how-to-show-sales-tax-on-an-invoice/).

## Use tax on your supplies

Packaging, equipment and supplies you buy for the business may be taxable. If a supplier did not charge tax, you may owe use tax. See [sales tax vs use tax](/blog/sales-tax-vs-use-tax/).

## FAQ

### Does Etsy collect sales tax for me?

Etsy and similar marketplaces generally collect and remit sales tax on orders placed through their platform in states where they are required to. Check the marketplace's help pages for the current list.

### Do I still need a sales tax permit?

Possibly. If you also sell outside the marketplace or your state requires registration, you may need one. See your state's rules.

### Do marketplace sales count toward my threshold?

In some states they do. Track them separately and check each state.

### Is this tax advice?

No. It is a general overview, and rules vary by state. Confirm with your state or a tax professional.
