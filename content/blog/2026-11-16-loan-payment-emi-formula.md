An EMI (equated monthly instalment) is the fixed amount you pay each month to repay a loan with interest. Knowing the formula helps you compare loans before you sign.

> **EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)**
>
> P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate, n = number of months

## How do you calculate an EMI step by step?

Example: a loan of **10,000** at **12% a year** for **12 months**.

1. Monthly rate: 12% ÷ 12 = 1% = **0.01**
2. (1 + r)ⁿ = 1.01¹² = **1.126825**
3. Numerator: 10,000 × 0.01 × 1.126825 = **112.68**
4. Denominator: 1.126825 − 1 = **0.126825**
5. EMI: 112.68 ÷ 0.126825 = **about 888.49**

## How much interest do you pay in total?

Total paid: 888.49 × 12 = **10,661.88**. Total interest: 10,661.88 − 10,000 = **about 661.88**.

## How do amount, rate and term change the payment?

| Loan | Rate | Term | Monthly payment (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10,000 | 12% | 12 months | 888.49 |
| 10,000 | 12% | 24 months | 470.73 |
| 10,000 | 8% | 12 months | 869.88 |

A longer term lowers the monthly payment but increases the total interest.

## Tips for comparing loans

- Compare the **total cost**, not just the monthly payment.
- Check for fees, such as arrangement or early repayment charges.
- Make sure you compare the same term and the same type of interest rate.

## FAQ

### What does EMI stand for?

Equated monthly instalment, the fixed monthly payment on a loan.

### Is the interest rate in the formula yearly or monthly?

The formula uses the monthly rate. Divide the annual rate by 12.

### Do all loans use this formula?

Many fixed-rate instalment loans do, but lenders can have different rules and fees. Check your loan agreement.

### Is this financial advice?

No. It explains the calculation. For borrowing decisions, speak to a qualified adviser.
