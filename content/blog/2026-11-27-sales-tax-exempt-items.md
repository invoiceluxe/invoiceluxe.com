Not everything you sell is taxed. States exempt certain items and certain buyers, and the list is different almost everywhere. Here is how exemptions work and how to handle them on an invoice.

## Two kinds of exemptions

1. **Exempt items.** The product itself is not taxed, such as many groceries or prescription medicine.
2. **Exempt buyers.** The buyer is not taxed, such as a nonprofit, a government agency or a business buying for resale.

## Items that are often exempt

| Category | Common treatment |
|---|---|
| Prescription medicine | Exempt in nearly all states |
| Groceries | Exempt or taxed at a reduced rate in many states |
| Clothing | Exempt in a few states, such as Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Minnesota, and exempt up to a price limit in others |
| Medical devices | Often exempt with limits |
| Some services | Not taxed in many states |

Prepared food, candy, soft drinks and restaurant meals are often taxed even where plain groceries are not. Check your state's definitions, because a product's category can change the answer.

## Exempt buyers

Some buyers can buy without paying sales tax if they give you the right paperwork:

- **Resellers** buying goods to resell. See [resale certificates explained](/blog/resale-certificate-explained/).
- **Nonprofit organizations** with an exemption certificate from the state.
- **Government agencies**, depending on the state.

Get the certificate **before** you skip the tax, and keep it on file. Without it, you may owe the tax yourself in an audit.

## A mixed receipt example at 7%

| Item | Price | Taxable? |
|---|---|---|
| Packaged groceries | $42.00 | No |
| Prepared meal | $12.00 | Yes |
| Shirt | $30.00 | Yes |

Taxable subtotal: 12 + 30 = $42.00. Tax: 42 × 0.07 = **$2.94**. Total: 84.00 + 2.94 = **$86.94**.

Your own state may treat some of these differently. For the method with several items, see [sales tax on multiple items](/blog/sales-tax-on-multiple-items/).

## How to show an exemption on an invoice

1. Mark the line or the customer as exempt.
2. Write the reason, for example "Resale" or "Exempt organization".
3. Add the certificate number if you have one.
4. Keep the certificate with your records.

The layout is in [how to show sales tax on an invoice](/blog/how-to-show-sales-tax-on-an-invoice/).

## Do not guess

Exemptions are narrow and specific. If you are not sure, ask your state's revenue department. It is easier than fixing an audit later.

## FAQ

### Is food exempt from sales tax?

Plain groceries are exempt or taxed at a lower rate in many states. Prepared food and restaurant meals are often taxed.

### Is clothing exempt from sales tax?

Only in some states, and some limit the exemption by price.

### Can a customer simply tell me they are exempt?

No. Ask for an exemption certificate and keep it on file.

### Is this tax advice?

No. It is a general overview. Rules differ by state and change.
