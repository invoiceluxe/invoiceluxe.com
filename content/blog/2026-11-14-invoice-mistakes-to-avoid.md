An invoice that is unclear or incomplete gives the customer an easy reason to wait. Fixing these ten mistakes will make your invoices faster to approve and faster to pay.

> **Before you send:** number, date, due date, amounts, tax, payment details, correct recipient.

## 1. No invoice number

Every invoice needs a unique number. Without one, accounting teams cannot record it. See [how to number invoices](/blog/how-to-number-invoices/).

## 2. No due date or unclear payment terms

"Pay soon" is not a date. State the exact due date and the terms. See [invoice payment terms](/blog/invoice-payment-terms/).

## 3. Wrong or missing customer details

Use the exact company name and address the customer gave you. Large companies often reject invoices that do not match their records.

## 4. Vague descriptions

"Services" tells the customer nothing. Describe what was done, with dates, quantities and rates.

## 5. Totals that do not add up

Re-check the arithmetic, tax and rounding before sending. See [how to calculate VAT](/blog/how-to-calculate-vat/).

## 6. Wrong tax treatment

Missing VAT details or the wrong rate can make the invoice invalid. See [what to include on a VAT invoice](/blog/what-to-include-on-a-vat-invoice/).

## 7. Missing payment details

Add your bank details, payment link or accepted methods so paying takes seconds.

## 8. No purchase order number

Many companies will not pay without their PO number. Ask for it up front and put it on the invoice.

## 9. Sending to the wrong person

Send the invoice to the accounts or payment contact, not only to the person you worked with. See [how to send an invoice](/blog/how-to-send-an-invoice-to-a-customer/).

## 10. Not following up

Most late payments are simply forgotten invoices. Send a polite reminder before and after the due date. See [payment reminder email](/blog/payment-reminder-email-overdue-invoice/).

## FAQ

### What is the most common invoice mistake?

Missing or unclear due dates, followed by wrong customer details.

### Can I correct an invoice after sending it?

Usually you issue a corrected invoice or a credit note. Rules differ by country, so check what applies to you.

### How fast should a customer pay an invoice?

It depends on your agreed terms, commonly 14 to 30 days.

### Is this legal advice?

No. It is a practical checklist. Check local requirements for your invoices.
