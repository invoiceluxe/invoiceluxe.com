Payment terms tell your client when and how they have to pay. Clear terms get you paid on time. Unclear ones lead to late payments and awkward emails.

![Common invoice payment terms from due on receipt to Net 60](/images/blog/invoice-payment-terms-example.jpg)

## Common payment terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Due on receipt | Payment is expected as soon as the invoice is received |
| Net 7 | Payment is due 7 days after the invoice date |
| Net 14 | Due within 14 days |
| Net 30 | Due within 30 days |
| Net 60 | Due within 60 days |
| 2/10 net 30 | 2% discount if paid within 10 days, otherwise the full amount within 30 days |

## Example: invoice dated October 4

- **Net 7** means the due date is October 11.
- **Net 30** means the due date is November 3.

Always write the actual due date on the invoice, not only the term, so there is no room for doubt.

## Early payment discounts

The term **2/10 net 30** rewards fast payers. On a $1,000 invoice:

- Paid within 10 days: $1,000 × 0.98 = **$980**
- Paid after day 10 and by day 30: **$1,000**

A discount like this can speed up cash flow, but it costs you 2% of the invoice. Use it only if getting paid sooner is worth that price.

## How to choose

- **Freelancers and small jobs:** due on receipt, Net 7 or Net 14.
- **Regular business clients:** Net 30 is a common standard.
- **Large companies:** they may insist on Net 45 or Net 60. Ask for a deposit or milestone payments if you cannot wait that long.
- **New clients:** shorter terms or a deposit up front reduce your risk.

## Late fees

If you want to charge a late fee or interest, state it on the invoice or in your contract before the work starts. Rules on late fees differ by place, so check your local requirements.

## What to put on the invoice

- The invoice date and the exact due date.
- The payment term, for example "Net 14".
- Your bank or payment details.
- Any late fee or early payment discount.

## FAQ

### What does Net 30 mean?

The client has 30 days from the invoice date to pay.

### Which payment term is best for freelancers?

Net 7 or Net 14 works well for most freelancers. For larger projects, ask for a deposit as well.

### What does 2/10 net 30 mean?

The client gets 2% off if they pay within 10 days. Otherwise the full amount is due within 30 days.

### Can I charge interest on late payments?

Often yes, if you agreed it in advance. Check your local rules and put it in writing.
