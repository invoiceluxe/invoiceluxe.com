A resale certificate lets a business buy items **without paying sales tax**, because the tax will be collected later when the item is sold to the final customer. It prevents the same product from being taxed twice.

## How it works

1. You buy inventory from a supplier.
2. You give the supplier a completed **resale certificate**, also called a resale exemption certificate.
3. The supplier does not charge sales tax on those items.
4. You collect sales tax from your customer when you resell the items.

## A worked example

A boutique buys $3,000 of clothing from a wholesaler to sell in its shop. At 7%, tax would be 3,000 × 0.07 = **$210.00**.

With a valid resale certificate, the boutique pays **$0.00** tax on that purchase. When it sells a $60 jacket, it collects tax from the customer at that point.

## What you need to get one

Most states require a **sales tax permit** (or seller's permit) before you can use a resale certificate. The certificate itself is usually a form from your state, and some states accept a multistate form. Read [do I need a sales tax permit](/blog/do-i-need-a-sales-tax-permit/).

## What goes on the certificate

- Buyer's name and address
- Sales tax permit number
- A description of what you will resell
- Signature and date
- The seller it is given to

## When you cannot use it

A resale certificate is only for items you will **resell** or include in a product you sell. Do not use it for:

- Office equipment, tools or computers you will use yourself
- Cleaning supplies, uniforms or other operating costs
- Personal purchases

If you buy something with a certificate and then use it yourself, you may owe **use tax**. See [sales tax vs use tax](/blog/sales-tax-vs-use-tax/).

## If you are the seller

When a customer says they are buying for resale, ask for the certificate **before** you skip the tax, check that it is complete and keep it on file. If you do not have it in an audit, the state may ask you to pay the tax. See [sales tax exemptions](/blog/sales-tax-exempt-items/).

## How to show it on an invoice

Mark the line as exempt, write "Resale" and add the certificate number. The layout is in [how to show sales tax on an invoice](/blog/how-to-show-sales-tax-on-an-invoice/).

## FAQ

### What is a resale certificate?

It is a form a business gives to its supplier to buy goods for resale without paying sales tax.

### Is a resale certificate the same as a sales tax permit?

No. The permit registers you to collect tax. The certificate is the paper you give a supplier to buy tax-free for resale.

### Can I use a resale certificate for supplies?

Generally no. Only for items you resell or that become part of what you sell.

### Is this tax advice?

No. Requirements differ by state. Check with your state's revenue department.
