Most freelancers hear "sales tax" and wonder whether it applies to them. The honest answer: **it depends on your state and on what you deliver.** Here is a simple way to decide.

## Goods are usually taxed, services often are not

In most US states, sales tax applies to the sale of physical goods. Services are taxed less broadly. Many states do not tax professional services such as consulting, writing or design. Some states tax certain services, such as repair, installation or specific digital services.

Because the lists differ by state, there is no single rule for every freelancer.

## Questions to ask

1. **What am I selling?** A service, a physical product, a digital file or a mix?
2. **Where is my client?** The buyer's location often decides which state's rules apply.
3. **Does my state tax this type of service?** Check your state revenue department's list of taxable services.
4. **Do I have a sales tax permit?** If you collect tax, you need one. See [do I need a sales tax permit](/blog/do-i-need-a-sales-tax-permit/).

## Examples

| Work | Often taxable? |
|---|---|
| Consulting or coaching | Often not |
| Graphic design (delivered digitally) | Depends on the state |
| Printed brochures delivered to the client | Often yes (tangible goods) |
| Website maintenance | Depends on the state |
| Equipment repair | Often yes in states that tax repairs |

Treat this table as a starting point, not a rule.

## What a mixed invoice looks like

Suppose you charge $2,500 for design work, which your state does not tax, and also sell $300 of printed materials taxed at 7%.

- Design work: $2,500.00 (no tax)
- Printed materials: $300.00
- Sales tax: 300 × 0.07 = $21.00
- **Total: $2,821.00**

Show the taxable and non-taxable lines separately so the client can see what the tax applies to. The layout is in [how to show sales tax on an invoice](/blog/how-to-show-sales-tax-on-an-invoice/).

## When your clients are in other states

Selling services to clients in other states can create nexus questions if the services are taxable there. Read [what is sales tax nexus](/blog/what-is-sales-tax-nexus/).

## Keep records

Keep copies of your invoices, the rate you charged and why a line was exempt. If you sell to a business that claims an exemption, ask for the exemption certificate and keep it on file.

## FAQ

### Do independent contractors charge sales tax?

Only if the work is taxable in the relevant state. Many pure services are not, but physical goods and some specific services are.

### Do I need to charge tax on digital files I deliver?

It depends on the state. Some tax digital goods and others do not. See [are digital products and SaaS taxable](/blog/are-digital-products-and-saas-taxable/).

### What if I charge tax by mistake?

Tell the client, issue a corrected invoice or a credit note, and refund the tax. See [how to correct an invoice](/blog/how-to-correct-an-invoice-credit-note/).

### Is this tax advice?

No. Confirm taxability with your state or a tax professional before you charge or skip tax.
