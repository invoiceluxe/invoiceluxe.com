A good quote does two jobs: it shows the customer exactly what they get for the price, and it makes saying yes easy. A vague or late quote is one of the quickest ways to lose a job.

> **A quote should answer three questions:** what will you do, how much will it cost, and what happens next.

## What should a quote include?

- **Your details and the customer's details**, with a quote number and the date.
- **A clear description of the work**, split into line items where possible.
- **Prices for each item**, tax shown separately, and the total.
- **A validity period**, for example "valid for 14 days".
- **Payment terms**: deposit, due date and accepted payment methods.
- **Timeline** and anything that is not included.
- **How to accept**: reply to the email, sign, or confirm in writing.

## What should the email say?

> **Subject:** Quote 2026-031 for your logo and branding project
>
> Hello Mark,
>
> Thank you for the conversation yesterday. Attached is quote 2026-031 for the logo and branding work. The total is £780.00 including VAT, and the quote is valid until 20 November.
>
> If it looks good, simply reply "accepted" and I will send the deposit invoice and start on Monday. I am happy to adjust anything before then.
>
> Kind regards,
> Your Name

## How long should a quote be valid?

Most freelancers use 14 to 30 days. Prices for materials, time or currency can change, so a validity date protects you and encourages a decision.

## How do you follow up on a quote?

Wait two to three days, then send a short, friendly message asking if they have questions. One more nudge after a week is reasonable. If the customer accepts, turn the quote into an invoice. For the next step read [how to send an invoice to a customer](/blog/how-to-send-an-invoice-to-a-customer/).

## Is a quote the same as an estimate?

Not exactly. A quote is a fixed price offer, while an estimate is an approximation that can change. Read [quote vs estimate vs invoice](/blog/quote-vs-estimate-vs-invoice/) for the full comparison.

## FAQ

### Is a quote legally binding?

It depends on your country and on how the quote is worded. In many places an accepted quote forms a contract. When in doubt, state the terms clearly or ask a professional.

### Should I charge for a quote?

Most small quotes are free. For complex projects you can charge a fee for detailed scoping and deduct it if the customer hires you.

### Can I change a quote after sending it?

Yes, but send a new version with a new date or version number so there is no confusion about which one applies.

### What if the customer says my price is too high?

Offer options instead of only a discount, for example a smaller scope, phased delivery or a different package.
