Unit conversions come up in travel, shipping, cooking, fitness and work. These are the four most common ones, with the exact formula and a quick example.

## How do you convert miles to kilometres?

> **Kilometres = miles × 1.609344**

Example: a marathon is 26.2 miles. 26.2 × 1.609344 = **42.16 km**.

| Miles | Kilometres |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1.61 |
| 5 | 8.05 |
| 10 | 16.09 |
| 26.2 | 42.16 |

To go the other way, divide by 1.609344 (or multiply km by 0.6214).

## How do you convert kilograms to pounds?

> **Pounds = kilograms × 2.20462**

Example: 70 kg × 2.20462 = **154.32 lb**. To convert pounds to kilograms, divide by 2.20462.

## How do you convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

> **°F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32**

Example: 20 °C × 9 ÷ 5 = 36, plus 32 = **68 °F**.

| °C | °F |
|---|---|
| 0 | 32 |
| 20 | 68 |
| 37 | 98.6 |
| 100 | 212 |

To convert back: °C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9.

## How do you convert inches to centimetres?

> **Centimetres = inches × 2.54**

Example: 12 inches × 2.54 = **30.48 cm**.

## Tips for accurate conversions

- Keep the full conversion factor until the last step, then round.
- Check the direction: multiply to go one way, divide to go back.
- For business use, such as shipping or product listings, state the unit clearly on invoices and quotes.

## FAQ

### How many kilometres is 1 mile?

1 mile is 1.609344 kilometres.

### How do I quickly convert km to miles in my head?

Multiply by 0.6 for a rough answer. For 100 km that gives about 60 miles; the exact figure is 62.1.

### Is 0 °C the same as 32 °F?

Yes. Water freezes at 0 °C, which is 32 °F.

### How many pounds are in a kilogram?

About 2.205 pounds.
