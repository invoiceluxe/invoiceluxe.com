When a customer pays late, you can sometimes charge interest or a fee. To do it fairly, you need a clear formula and terms that were agreed before the work started.

> **Interest = invoice amount × annual rate × days late ÷ 365**

## How do you calculate late payment interest?

Example: an invoice of 2,000 is 30 days late and the agreed annual rate is 8%.

2,000 × 0.08 × 30 ÷ 365 = **13.15**

Another example: 5,000 at 10% for 45 days is 5,000 × 0.10 × 45 ÷ 365 = **61.64**.

| Invoice | Annual rate | Days late | Interest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | 8% | 30 | 6.58 |
| 2,000 | 8% | 30 | 13.15 |
| 5,000 | 10% | 45 | 61.64 |
| 10,000 | 8% | 60 | 131.51 |

## Late fee or interest?

- **Interest** grows with the number of days late.
- **A flat fee** is a fixed amount added once the invoice is overdue.

You can use either or both, as long as they are allowed where you work and agreed with the customer.

## Is it legal to charge late fees?

Rules differ by country and by type of customer. Some countries, including the UK for business-to-business debts, have statutory late payment rights. Other places require the terms to be in your contract. Check the rules that apply to you or ask a professional.

## How do you make late fees enforceable?

1. **Put them in writing** on your quote, contract and invoice.
2. **State the rate and the starting date**, for example "interest at 8% per year from the day after the due date".
3. **Be consistent.** Apply the same terms to every customer.

See [invoice payment terms](/blog/invoice-payment-terms/) for wording you can reuse.

## Should you actually charge it?

Often the threat of a fee is enough, and a polite reminder works better than a bill for interest. Many freelancers keep the clause in their terms and only use it for repeat late payers. Start with a [payment reminder email](/blog/payment-reminder-email-overdue-invoice/).

## FAQ

### How is late payment interest calculated per day?

Invoice amount × annual rate ÷ 365 gives the interest for one day.

### Can I add a late fee after the invoice has been sent?

Only if the terms were agreed beforehand. Adding new charges later can cause disputes.

### Do I charge VAT on late payment interest?

Rules differ. Ask your tax authority or an accountant.

### Is this legal or financial advice?

No. It explains the calculation. Check your local rules and contract terms.
