These three documents are often mixed up, but each has a different job. Using the right one avoids disputes and gets you paid faster.

> **Estimate:** roughly what it will cost. **Quote:** the price I offer. **Invoice:** please pay this amount.

## What is an estimate?

An estimate is an approximate price based on what you know now. The final cost can change if the work turns out bigger or smaller. Use it when the scope is unclear, for example repairs or a project that is still being defined.

## What is a quote?

A quote is a fixed price offer for a defined piece of work. If the customer accepts it, you agree to do the work at that price. Use it when the scope is clear. See [how to send a quote](/blog/how-to-send-a-quote-or-estimate/).

## What is an invoice?

An invoice is a request for payment for work done or goods supplied. It lists what was delivered, the tax, the total, the due date and how to pay. See [how to create an invoice](/blog/how-to-create-an-invoice/).

## Comparison table

| | Estimate | Quote | Invoice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Give a rough price | Offer a fixed price | Request payment |
| Sent | Before work | Before work | After work or at a milestone |
| Price can change | Yes | Usually not | No |
| Has a due date | No | Validity date | Payment due date |
| Needs a number | Optional | Recommended | Yes |

## How do they work together?

1. The customer asks for a price. You send an **estimate** or a **quote**.
2. The customer accepts. You do the work.
3. You send an **invoice** that matches the accepted quote.
4. After payment you can issue a **receipt**. See [how to write a receipt](/blog/how-to-write-a-receipt/).

Keep the numbers consistent. If the invoice total differs from the quote, explain why, for example approved extra work.

## Which should you use?

- Clear scope and fixed price: quote
- Unclear scope: estimate, then a quote once the scope is known
- Work finished or milestone reached: invoice

## Create all three

Build a quote, then turn the details into an invoice and send it.

## FAQ

### Is an estimate legally binding?

Usually not, but local rules vary. Make clear that it is an estimate and what could change it.

### Can I turn a quote into an invoice?

Yes. Copy the items and prices, add the invoice number, date and due date, and send it when the work is done.

### Do I need a quote before an invoice?

No, but it prevents disagreements about price.

### Is this legal advice?

No. For contracts and disputes, check the rules for your country or ask a professional.
