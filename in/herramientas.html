Free tools for invoicing and business management (India)

Invoice Luxe offers a complete set of free tools for freelancers and SMEs in India: create professional invoices, generate estimates/quotes, calculate GST online, track tax, and manage clients from one panel. No signup, no cost.

🧮 Online GST Calculator

Use the online GST calculator to add or remove tax instantly (INR, USD, GBP, EUR). Easy Mode guides you step by step — ideal for freelancers and small businesses.

📋 Estimate / Quote Generator

Create professional estimates and quotes quickly with GST, discount and total, then download or print from the browser.

📄 PDF Engine and invoicing

Create and sign PDF documents for free. The invoice generator lets you edit layout, add a digital signature and export instantly.

📊 Tax Analytics

Track your obligations via the tax analytics panel: tax due, net profit and sales volume.

FAQ

Are Invoice Luxe tools free?
Yes, all tools are free and require no signup.
How do I calculate GST online?
Open the online GST calculator, choose add or remove tax, enter amount and rate.
Is my data safe?
Yes, processing is local and your data never leaves your device.
