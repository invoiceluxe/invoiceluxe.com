Income & Expense Tracker for Freelancers and Small Businesses in India

Pocket Invoice Wallet is a free business finance tool for India that tracks your income and expenses with automatic GST calculation. Amounts are in Indian Rupees (₹), give you a real-time financial overview, and let you export a PDF report instantly — no registration required. Invoice archive income is included automatically.

How to use Wallet

  1. Choose Income or Expense using the toggle above the form.
  2. Enter a description, an optional client name, and the amount.
  3. Select the GST rate that applies to this transaction, or enter a custom rate.
  4. Tap Save Transaction to add it to your records.
  5. Review your totals and GST position at the top of the page at any time.
  6. Tap Get PDF Report to generate a shareable summary of your finances.

Why use Invoice Luxe Wallet

  • Automatic GST split on every transaction, so you always know what you owe or can reclaim.
  • Multi-currency support for international freelancers and businesses.
  • All data stays on your device — no signup, no tracking.
  • Works alongside the rest of the Invoice Luxe toolset (invoicing, PDF tools, tax analytics).
