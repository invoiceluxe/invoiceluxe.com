Advanced VAT Calculator & Income Tracker

Whether you are a freelancer, a small business owner, or just doing personal accounting, calculating taxes shouldn't be complicated. This free and dynamic tool helps you instantly calculate Net, Gross, and VAT amounts with a single click.

What is the "VAT ANALYSE" Feature?

Clicking the gold VAT ANALYSE button at the top of the calculator opens a dedicated financial dashboard in a new window within the page. In this special screen, you can:

  • List your multiple Incomes (Green) and Expenses (Red).
  • Apply different VAT rates to different entries.
  • Automatically see your total payable or receivable tax balance.
  • Export the entire detailed financial report instantly via WhatsApp.

Step-by-Step User Guide

  • Basic Calculation: Use the keypad just like a standard calculator. The result appears on the green display.
  • Set Tax Rate: Enter your country's or product's VAT percentage into the small gold input box (default is 21%).
  • VAT INCL (Included): Click this if the amount you entered already contains tax. It will extract the pure net price and tax amount.
  • VAT EXCL (Excluded): Click this if you want to add tax on top of your pure net amount.
  • Data Safety: All your calculations and lists are saved locally on your browser. Even if you close the page, your data remains intact!
