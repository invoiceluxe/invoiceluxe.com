📄 How to Create a Professional Invoice in India

Invoice Luxe is a free, easy mobile app that lets you create professional PDF invoices in seconds — no account or subscription required. Track your working hours and run your business the smart way.

✅ Invoice Luxe Highlights

  • Create and download PDF invoices directly from your phone
  • Save customer details and reuse them anytime
  • Set GST rates easily (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%)
  • Include bank details, IBAN and tax number
  • Available in 22+ languages, including English (India)
  • All data stays on your phone — no cloud, full privacy
  • ⏱️ Track working hours — perfect for hourly billing
  • 🏷️ Discount calculator, PDF generator and HTML editor also included

⏱️ Why a Working Hours Tracker Matters

If you're a freelancer, contractor, or service provider, logging your working hours is essential. Our Work Hours Tracker tool helps you note the time spent on every project and build accurate invoices.

🛠️ Other Useful Tools

  • Discount Engine — calculate discounts instantly
  • PDF Engine — turn any document into a PDF
  • HTML Editor — edit code live
  • Calendar — manage your schedule and meetings

🙋 Frequently Asked Questions

Is Invoice Luxe completely free?
Yes, Invoice Luxe is 100% free. There are no hidden fees or subscriptions.
Can I create an invoice with GST?
Absolutely! You can set any custom GST rate (like 18% or 28%) to prepare a GST-inclusive invoice.
How does the working hours tracker work?
Just start the timer when you begin work and stop it when you're done. All hours are logged and you can add them straight to an invoice.
Is my data safe?
Yes. All information is saved only on your device — no server, no cloud.
Does this app work on Android and iOS?
Yes, it's a PWA (Progressive Web App) that runs in any smartphone browser and can be installed to your home screen.
