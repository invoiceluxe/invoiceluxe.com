Invoice Luxe: Free Online Invoice Generator, PDF Signature & Business Tools

Invoice Luxe is a free online invoice generator for freelancers, contractors and small businesses. Create professional PDF invoices in seconds, calculate taxes with the built-in VAT calculator, sign PDF documents online, create PDFs from scratch or convert images to PDF — with no registration, no hidden fees and nothing to install.

Available in English, Turkish, Dutch, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese, Russian, Hindi, Japanese and Arabic. Invoice Luxe is a progressive web app (PWA) that runs on iPhone, Android, Windows and Mac: install it to your home screen and keep working offline.

User Manual & Feature Guide
Get the most out of your dashboard with these core features:
  1. Customizable dashboard: open the tools menu (top-right apps button) to switch shortcut tiles such as the unit converter or calendar tools on or off.
  2. Smart sorting: reorder your pinned tools with the up and down buttons to match your daily priorities.
  3. Floating VAT terminal: open the calculator (gold button, bottom-right) for fast tax math with Gross and Net modes.
  4. Day notes: tap any day in the weekly calendar to save a note. The bell shows your upcoming notes.
  5. Private balance: tap the eye icon to hide or show your balance on screen.

How to create a free invoice online

  1. Open Create Invoice and add your business and client details.
  2. Add your line items, choose the VAT rate and check the totals.
  3. Export the invoice as a PDF, then find it later in your invoice archive.

Frequently asked questions

Is Invoice Luxe really free?
Yes. Invoice Luxe and all of its business tools are free to use, with no registration and no hidden fees.
How do I sign a PDF online for free?
Open the PDF Engine, load or create your PDF, add your signature, place it on the page and download the signed PDF. No account is needed.
How can I create a PDF online?
Use the PDF Engine to build a document with text, images and a signature, then export it as a PDF. To make an invoice, open Create Invoice, fill in your details and export the invoice as a PDF.
Can I convert an image (JPG, PNG) to PDF?
Yes. Add your JPG, PNG or photo in the PDF Engine and save it as a PDF. You can crop or compress the picture first in the Image Studio.
Does Invoice Luxe work on my phone and offline?
Yes. It is a progressive web app: use it in any browser, or tap Get the App to install it on your iPhone, Android phone or computer and keep working offline.
How do I calculate VAT from a gross or net amount?
Open the floating calculator, choose Gross (price includes VAT) or Net (price excludes VAT), enter your VAT rate and the amount, and read net, VAT and total instantly.
Which languages does Invoice Luxe support?
English, Turkish, Dutch, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese, Russian, Hindi, Japanese and Arabic.
