Free VAT Calculator & US Sales Tax Calculator

"VAT calculator" is one of the most searched financial tool queries in the world, and it is exactly why we built Invoice Luxe's calculator to work everywhere — including the United States, where the concept is different but the need is the same.

How our VAT calculator works

Enter any price and any tax rate, and switch between two modes: Gross → Net (the price already includes VAT and you want to see the VAT amount and the net price), or Net → Gross (you have a price before tax and want to add VAT to get the final total). This covers every common search — "add VAT to a price", "remove VAT from a price", "VAT percentage calculator", "reverse VAT calculator" — in one free tool.

VAT vs. US sales tax — one calculator, both systems

The United States does not use VAT; it uses state and local sales tax, calculated only at the final point of sale rather than at every stage of production like VAT in the UK, the EU and most of the world. Because rates and rules differ by state, county and even city, Invoice Luxe does not hard-code a single US rate — instead, our sales tax calculator mode lets American freelancers and small businesses enter their own local sales tax percentage and instantly see the tax amount and total, exactly the way our international users calculate VAT. It is the same trusted engine behind both the "VAT calculator" and "sales tax calculator" searches, unified in one free app.

Built for invoicing, not just quick sums

Unlike a plain calculator page, our VAT and sales tax engine is wired directly into the invoice generator and tax analytics dashboard, so every line item on an invoice, every discount, and every period total already reflects the correct tax treatment — useful for EU VAT-registered freelancers, UK sole traders, Canadian GST/HST filers and US small businesses reporting sales tax alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a VAT calculator and how does it work?

It works out the Value Added Tax portion of a price. In Gross mode you enter a price that already includes VAT and see the net price and VAT amount; in Net mode you enter a price before tax and add VAT at any rate to get the gross total. Invoice Luxe does both instantly in your browser.

Does Invoice Luxe support US sales tax as well as VAT?

Yes. The US uses state and local sales tax instead of VAT, so American users enter their own local rate the same way international users enter a VAT rate — one free calculator, both systems.

Is Invoice Luxe really free, and do I need to sign up?

Yes, 100% free, with no signup, no subscription and no hidden paywall on any tool, including the invoice generator and the VAT / sales tax calculator.

What languages does Invoice Luxe support?

22 languages and country editions, including dedicated English editions for the US, UK, Canada and India — see the full list above.

Where is my invoice and customer data stored?

Locally, in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded to our servers unless you choose to export or share it yourself.
