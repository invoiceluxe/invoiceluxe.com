Quick guide

  1. Enter at least name (company or person).
  2. Add address, city and country — type any country name; it is saved as you write it.
  3. Optional: email, phone, GST/HST, tax ID.
  4. Tap Save. Edit or delete anytime. Clients appear in the invoice form.

Required: name. Country can be typed freely (any country / custom text).
