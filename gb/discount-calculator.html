InvoiceLuxe Online Discount Calculator – Sale Price & Savings Guide (UK)

Welcome to the free InvoiceLuxe Discount Calculator – a fast Progressive Web App for shoppers and retailers in the United Kingdom. Calculate percent off, sale price and total savings in seconds. No signup, works offline.

1. How to Use the Discount Calculator

  1. Choose Easy Mode or Calculator — Easy Mode uses simple fields. Calculator Mode offers a classic keypad with live breakdown.
  2. Enter Original (List) Price — The retail price before discount.
  3. Set Discount Percentage — Tap quick chips (10%, 20%, 30%, 50%) or enter a custom rate.
  4. See Savings & Final Price — Instantly view how much you save and what you pay.

2. Discount Formulas

  • Sale Price = Original Price − [Original Price × (Discount % ÷ 100)]
  • Savings Amount = Original Price × (Discount % ÷ 100)
  • Original Price (reverse) = Sale Price ÷ [1 − (Discount % ÷ 100)]

Example – £90 item with 20% off

  • Original Price: £90.00
  • Total Savings: £18.00
  • Final Price: £72.00

3. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find the original price if I only know the sale price and discount?

Divide the sale price by [1 − (Discount % ÷ 100)]. Example: £80 after 20% off → 80 ÷ 0.80 = £100 original.

Is InvoiceLuxe free?

Yes. All calculators are 100% free, require no registration and work offline as a PWA.
