Income & Expense Tracker for Freelancers and Small Businesses

Invoice Luxe Wallet is a free business finance tool that tracks your income and expenses with automatic VAT calculation. It supports multiple currencies (EUR, USD, GBP, TRY and more), gives you a real-time financial overview, and lets you export a PDF report instantly — no registration required.

How to use Wallet

  1. Choose Income or Expense using the toggle above the form.
  2. Enter a description, an optional client name, and the amount.
  3. Select the VAT rate that applies to this transaction, or enter a custom rate.
  4. Tap Save Transaction to add it to your records.
  5. Review your totals and VAT position at the top of the page at any time.
  6. Tap Get PDF Report to generate a shareable summary of your finances.

Why use Invoice Luxe Wallet

  • Automatic VAT split on every transaction, so you always know what you owe or can reclaim.
  • Multi-currency support for international freelancers and businesses.
  • All data stays on your device — no signup, no tracking.
  • Works alongside the rest of the Invoice Luxe toolset (invoicing, PDF tools, tax analytics).
