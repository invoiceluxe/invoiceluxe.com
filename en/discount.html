Online Discount Calculator – Sale Price & Savings

This free discount calculator is a web app that runs right in your browser on any phone, tablet or computer – nothing to install and no signup. Enter the original price, choose a percentage off, and it instantly shows the final sale price and how much you save.

Use the quick keypad for fast shop-floor checks or the manual entry mode to see every step of the calculation. The app is available in 13 languages.

How to Use the Discount Calculator

  1. Enter Price: Enter the original tag price using the keypad or the input field.
  2. Select Discount: Pick a percentage preset (10%, 20%, 30%, 50%) or type a custom rate.
  3. See Savings: Instantly view your final discounted price and total savings.

Discount Formula

Savings = Original price × (Discount % ÷ 100). Final price = Original price − Savings. Example: a $80 item with 25% off saves $20 (80 × 0.25) and costs $60.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a discount percentage?

Multiply the original price by the discount rate divided by 100 to get the savings, then subtract the savings from the original price. For 30% off $50: 50 × 0.30 = $15 saved, so you pay $35.

Is this discount calculator free to use?

Yes. It is a free web app that works in any modern browser on mobile and desktop. There is nothing to download and no account is required.

Can I enter a custom discount rate?

Yes. Pick a preset such as 10%, 20%, 30% or 50%, or type any rate from 0 to 100 in the discount field.
