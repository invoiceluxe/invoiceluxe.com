Quote vs estimate vs invoice

A quote is a fixed price offer that a client can accept. An estimate is an approximate cost that may change. An invoice is a request for payment after the work is agreed or done. Once a client accepts your quote, you can turn it into an invoice.

Tips for a quote that wins work

Be specific about what is included, show the total clearly, add a validity date so prices do not stay open forever, and state payment terms such as a deposit. Short and clear quotes get accepted faster.

Frequently asked questions

Is a quote legally binding?

It depends on your country and wording. In many places an accepted quote forms a contract, so include scope, price, validity and payment terms.

Can I add a discount and tax?

Yes. Enter a discount % and tax % and the total updates. The tax is calculated after the discount.

How do I send the quote?

Print or save it as PDF and attach it to an email.
