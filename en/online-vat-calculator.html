How to Calculate VAT (Formulas)

The maths behind a VAT calculation is simple once you know which number you start with.

How to add VAT to a price

Gross = Net × (1 + VAT rate ÷ 100)

VAT amount = Gross − Net

Example: a net price of 100 with 20% VAT: 100 × 1.20 = 120. The VAT is 20.

How to remove VAT from a price (reverse VAT)

Net = Gross ÷ (1 + VAT rate ÷ 100)

VAT amount = Gross − Net

Example: a VAT-inclusive price of 120 with 20% VAT: 120 ÷ 1.20 = 100. The VAT is 20. A very common mistake is subtracting 20% of 120 (which gives 96) – that is wrong, because VAT is charged on the net amount, not the gross.

How to find the VAT rate

VAT rate = (Gross ÷ Net − 1) × 100

If an item costs 50 before tax and 60 after tax, the rate is (60 ÷ 50 − 1) × 100 = 20%.

VAT Inclusive vs VAT Exclusive Prices

A VAT-exclusive (net, ex-VAT) price does not include tax. A VAT-inclusive (gross, inc-VAT) price already contains it. B2B suppliers usually quote prices excluding VAT, while consumer shops in the UK, EU, Australia and many other countries show prices including VAT. Knowing which one you are looking at is essential before you use any VAT calculator.

Sales Tax Calculator for the USA

The United States does not have a national VAT. Instead, sales tax is set by states and local jurisdictions and is normally added at checkout. To use this tool as a US sales tax calculator, choose "United States – custom" and type your local rate. Because US sales tax is added on top of the listed price, the "Add VAT" mode is the one you will use most.

GST Calculator for Australia, Canada, New Zealand and Singapore

Goods and Services Tax works like VAT. In Australia the GST is 10%, so a GST-inclusive price divided by 11 gives the GST portion. In New Zealand it is 15%, in Singapore 9%, and in Canada 5% federal GST with additional provincial rates (HST in some provinces). Pick the matching option above and the GST calculator will fill in the rate for you.

Tips for Accurate VAT Calculations

  • Confirm whether your starting figure is net or gross before you calculate.
  • Use the correct rate for the product or service – standard, reduced or zero.
  • Round VAT to two decimal places only at the end, per invoice line or per invoice depending on your local rules.
  • Keep invoices showing the net amount, VAT rate, VAT amount and gross total.
  • Use the manual mode to double-check totals on supplier invoices.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate VAT?

Multiply the net price by (1 + rate). At 20% that means × 1.20. Subtract the net from the result to get the VAT amount.

How do I remove VAT from a price?

Divide the VAT-inclusive price by (1 + rate). At 20% divide by 1.20. Do not just take 20% off.

What is the current UK VAT rate?

The UK standard rate is 20%, with a 5% reduced rate and a 0% zero rate for certain goods and services.

Is VAT the same as sales tax or GST?

They are similar consumption taxes. VAT and GST are collected at every stage of the supply chain with input credits, while US sales tax is charged at the final sale to the consumer.
