Formula

Revenue needed = (Take-home + Expenses) / (1 - Tax rate)
Billable hours = Hours per week x (52 - Weeks off)
Hourly rate = Revenue needed / Billable hours

Why billable hours matter

You cannot bill every hour you work. Admin, marketing, sales calls and unpaid revisions all take time. Most freelancers bill 20-30 hours a week even when working full time, so base your rate on billable hours, not on a 40-hour week.

Example

Goal: $60,000 take-home, $6,000 expenses, 25% tax, 25 billable hours a week and 6 weeks off. Revenue needed is $88,000. Billable hours are 1,150. The rate is about $76.52 per hour.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my freelance hourly rate?

Add your desired income and business expenses, adjust for tax, then divide by the number of hours you can realistically bill in a year.

Should I include taxes in my rate?

Yes. The tax rate lifts the revenue you must earn so that your take-home income still matches your goal after tax is paid.

Is a project price better than an hourly rate?

Many freelancers quote projects by value and use the hourly rate as a floor to make sure a project is worth the time.
