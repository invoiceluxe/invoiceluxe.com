Free Business Tools for Freelancers & Small Businesses – US, UK & Worldwide

Invoice Luxe is a 100% free progressive web app (PWA) built for freelancers, contractors, sole traders and small businesses in the United States, the United Kingdom and worldwide. No tool requires registration, subscription or hidden fees. Everything runs in your browser; you can also install the app on your iPhone, Android phone or computer and keep working offline. Pin your favourite tools to the top of this page so they are one tap away.

Why choose Invoice Luxe tools?

  • Completely free: Invoice generator, PDF sign & edit, VAT & sales tax calculator, tax analytics, work time logger and more – never any cost.
  • No registration needed: Start immediately. Your data stays on your device. No account, no tracking, no sharing with third parties.
  • Progressive Web App: Add to home screen on iOS or Android, open like a native app, work offline.
  • Mobile and desktop ready: iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows, Mac – the same experience everywhere.
  • US & UK friendly: Built for American freelancers (sales tax, invoices) and British sole traders (VAT, HMRC-ready workflows), plus international users.
  • Favorites: Star a tool to pin it to the top. Remove whenever you want. Preferences are stored only on your device.
  • Multilingual: English (US/UK), Turkish, German, French, Dutch, Italian and more.

Popular free tools for the US and UK

Invoice generator: Create professional PDF invoices for clients in the US or UK, keep a customer register and store them in the archive. PDF Engine & Sign PDF: Sign PDFs online, create PDFs from scratch, convert JPG/PNG to PDF, edit text and resize documents. VAT & Sales Tax Calculator: Split VAT from gross or net amounts (UK) or handle sales tax (US) instantly. Tax Analytics: Track VAT payable, VAT receivable and net profit. Work Time Logger: Log billable hours and turn them into invoices. Document Scanner: Scan receipts and contracts with your phone camera.

Install on Android

Add Invoice Luxe to your home screen. Free, no Play Store needed. Works offline.

android Chrome (recommended)

  1. Open invoiceluxe.com in the Chrome browser on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots) at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm. The Invoice Luxe icon appears on your home screen.
  5. Open it whenever you want – it works like a normal app and can work offline.

Tip

  1. Some phones show a banner at the bottom: “Add Invoice Luxe to Home screen”. You can use that too.
  2. If you don’t see Install, make sure you are using Chrome and the site is loaded over HTTPS.

Add to Home Screen on iOS

Install Invoice Luxe on iPhone or iPad. Use Safari – other browsers on iOS do not support this the same way.

phone_iphone iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open invoiceluxe.com in the Safari browser (not Chrome or Firefox).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (square with upward arrow).
  3. Scroll through the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. You can change the name if you want, then tap Add.
  5. The Invoice Luxe icon appears on your home screen. Open it like any other app.

Tip

  1. On iPad the Share button is often in the top toolbar.
  2. Once added, the app can open full-screen and work offline for many features.

How to use the Tools page

Short guide for favorites, categories and installing the app.

1. Browse by category

  1. Tools are grouped: Invoicing & Clients, PDF & Documents, Tax & Finance, Time & Planning, Utilities.
  2. Tap a card to open that tool. No registration required.

2. Pin favorites to the top

  1. Tap the star on a tool card to add it to Your favorites.
  2. Favorited tools stay pinned at the top of this page.
  3. Tap the star again to remove. Your choices are stored only on this device.

3. Add to Home Screen

  1. Use the bar at the top of this page, or open Profile (bottom right) for Android and iOS step-by-step guides.
  2. Once installed, open Invoice Luxe like a normal app and work offline where supported.

4. Dark / Light mode

  1. Open Profile (navigation bottom right) and use the Dark / Light switch.
  2. The preference is stored on your device.
