Netplus Ledger Luxe Edition — Expense Tracker & Invoice Generator

Netplus Ledger is a free income and expense tracker with a built-in PDF invoice generator. Log daily transactions on the calendar, see income vs. expense breakdowns with automatic VAT calculation for company profiles, and export or share professional statements as PDF or via WhatsApp.

Key features

  • Calendar-based income & expense logging for individuals or companies
  • Automatic VAT / net breakdown for corporate profiles
  • One-click PDF invoice and statement generation
  • Share statements instantly via WhatsApp or native share
  • Multiple profiles, multiple currencies, 13 interface languages
