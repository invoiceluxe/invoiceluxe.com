HOW TO USE WORK CALC?

Work Calc Work Time Logger is a free, professional online tool to track work hours seamlessly.

Work Calc allows you to record your daily clock-in/clock-out times, break durations, and work locations all on a single screen. Calculated net working times are automatically summed up, converted into a PDF report, and can be shared directly with your employer or client via WhatsApp.
