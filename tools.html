Free Business Tools for Freelancers & Small Businesses – US, UK & Worldwide

Invoice Luxe is a 100% free progressive web app (PWA) built for freelancers, contractors, sole traders and small businesses in the United States, United Kingdom and across the globe. No tool requires registration, subscription or hidden fees. Everything runs in your browser; you can also install the app on your iPhone, Android phone or computer and keep working offline. Pin your favourite tools to the top of this page so they stay one tap away.

Why choose Invoice Luxe tools?

  • Completely free: Invoice generator, sign PDF online, VAT & sales tax calculator, tax analytics, work time logger and more – no cost, ever.
  • No signup needed: Start instantly. Your data stays on your device. No account, no tracking, no third-party sharing.
  • Progressive Web App: Add to Home Screen on iOS or Android, open like a native app, work offline.
  • Mobile and desktop ready: iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows, Mac – the same experience everywhere.
  • US & UK friendly: Built for American freelancers (sales tax, invoices) and UK sole traders (VAT, HMRC-ready workflows), plus international users.
  • Favorites: Star any tool to pin it to the top. Remove anytime. Preferences are saved on your device.
  • Multilingual: English (US/UK), Turkish, German, French, Dutch, Italian and more.

Popular free tools for the US and UK

Invoice Generator: Create professional PDF invoices for clients in the US or UK, keep a client registry and store them in the archive. PDF Engine: Sign PDFs online, create PDFs from scratch, convert JPG/PNG to PDF. VAT & Sales Tax Calculator: Split VAT from gross or net amounts (UK) or handle sales tax (US) instantly. Tax Analytics: Track VAT to pay, VAT to receive and net profit. Work Time Logger: Log billable hours and turn them into invoices.

Install on Android

Add Invoice Luxe to your home screen. Free, no Play Store required. Works offline.

android Chrome (recommended)

  1. Open invoiceluxe.com in the Chrome browser on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots) in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm. The Invoice Luxe icon appears on your home screen.
  5. Open it anytime – it works like a normal app and can work offline.

Tip

  1. Some phones show a banner at the bottom: “Add Invoice Luxe to Home screen”. You can use that too.
  2. If you do not see Install, make sure you are using Chrome and the site is loaded over HTTPS.

Add to Home Screen on iOS

Install Invoice Luxe on iPhone or iPad. Use Safari – other browsers on iOS do not support this the same way.

phone_iphone iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open invoiceluxe.com in the Safari browser (not Chrome or Firefox).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (square with an upward arrow).
  3. Scroll the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. You can edit the name if you want, then tap Add.
  5. The Invoice Luxe icon appears on your home screen. Open it like any other app.

Tip

  1. On iPad the Share button is often in the top toolbar.
  2. Once added, the app can open full-screen and work offline for many features.

How to use the Tools page

Quick guide to favorites, categories and installing the app.

1. Browse by category

  1. Tools are grouped: Invoicing & Clients, PDF & Image, Tax & Finance, Time & Planning, Utility Tools.
  2. Tap any card to open that tool. No signup required.

2. Pin favorites to the top

  1. Tap the star on any tool card to add it to Your Favorites.
  2. Favorited tools stay pinned at the top of this page.
  3. Tap the star again to remove. Your choices are saved on this device only.

3. Add to Home Screen

  1. Use the bar at the top of this page, or open Profile (bottom right) for Android and iOS step-by-step guides.
  2. Once installed, open Invoice Luxe like a normal app and work offline where supported.

4. Dark / Light mode

  1. Open Profile (bottom right nav) and use the Dark / Light toggle.
  2. The preference is saved on your device.
