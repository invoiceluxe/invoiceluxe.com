Free QR Code Generator for Business, Personal and Payments

QR Studio Luxe is a free online QR code generator designed for individuals, creators, freelancers and businesses. Create professional QR codes for websites, digital business cards, contact information, WhatsApp, Wi-Fi networks, locations, bank details, Bitcoin payments and more.

Everything can be generated directly in your browser. You can preview your QR code, customize its appearance, download it and save your projects in your local QR archive.

Create a Professional Business QR Code

A business QR code can contain company information, contact details, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, WhatsApp numbers and physical locations. Use one combined business QR code or generate individual QR codes for specific purposes.

  • Business vCard QR codes
  • Company website QR codes
  • Phone and email QR codes
  • WhatsApp QR codes
  • Business location QR codes
  • Digital contact information

Bitcoin and Crypto Payment QR Codes

Create a Bitcoin payment QR code by entering a Bitcoin address, optional BTC amount, recipient label and payment message. The generator creates a Bitcoin URI that compatible wallet applications can interpret.

Crypto address QR codes can also be used for Ethereum and other URI-compatible cryptocurrency payment formats.

Bank and IBAN QR Codes

Store bank payment information such as account holder, bank name, IBAN, BIC or SWIFT information, amount, currency and payment reference. This is useful for invoices, businesses, freelancers and payment requests.

QR Code Archive

Your saved QR projects are stored locally in your browser. You can return later to preview, edit, duplicate or delete your saved QR codes. No account is required for the local archive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is QR Studio Luxe free?

Yes. The QR generator can be used directly in your browser without requiring an account.

Can I create a Bitcoin QR code?

Yes. Enter a Bitcoin address and optionally add an amount, label and payment message.

Can I create a vCard QR code?

Yes. Personal and business contact details can be combined into a digital vCard QR code.

Can I save my QR codes?

Yes. QR projects can be saved to the browser's local storage and managed from the archive.

Can I download my QR code?

Yes. You can export QR codes as PNG, SVG and PDF.
