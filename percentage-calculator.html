How percentages work

A percentage is a number out of 100. To find 15% of 200, multiply 200 by 0.15 to get 30. To turn a result back into a percent, divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100.

Percentage change example

A price rising from 80 to 100 is a change of (100 - 80) / 80 x 100 = 25% increase. Going back from 100 to 80 is only a 20% decrease, because the starting value is different.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number. 15% of 200 is 0.15 x 200 = 30.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100.

Why is a 20% rise not undone by a 20% fall?

The second percentage applies to a larger number, so it removes more than the first added. A 20% rise followed by a 20% fall leaves you 4% lower.
